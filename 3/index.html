Ecosystems and Wildlife App Proposal

Topic Description:

Topic: Ecosystems and Wildlife

Learning the different ecologies of the environments that surround us is incredibly important to understanding the world around us. From what goes on in the microscopic scale to what happens on a global level, it all revolves around ecology. Getting to know the intricacies of the different ecosystems and biomes can help in a very direct sense. This means learning what weather patterns are most common around where you live or where you are going, wildlife around you, or learning how to balance the nutrients in your diet according to your own chemical needs. This aspect of biology is my major, so I have a special interest in this field. This is why I am putting together this collection of different ecological factors in Mississippi, to compile and practice my knowledge of the ecosystems in our state.

Learning about the ecosystems and wildlife in the surrounding environments can be as interesting as it is important. The aim of this website is to provide an interesting and effective solution to informing the reader about the ecology of their area, this area specifically being Mississippi. Previously, there were not many databases put together for the average user to learn about the ecology of Mississippi. Typically, they are put together by the government for use by other government employees, such as the Mississippi Geospatial Data Catalogue. This website aims to provide accurate information to the average reader in a way that will encourage them to learn more about their environment.
