Red-Bellied Woodpecker

    They are omnivorous birds native to much of southern Mississippi, as well as much of the southeast U.S.
  • Their lifespans can range up to 4 years.
  • They can grow up to 0.09 lbs.
  • Despite their name, the most notable feature on the Red-Bellied Woodpecker is their bright red plum on their crown and back of neck. The red on their chest is very subtle in comparison.
