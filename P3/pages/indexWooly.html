Wooly Bear Caterpillar

    This little guy is an herbivore that sports fuzzy black and red stripes that attempt to deceive predators into thinking they are poisonous.
  • Also known as a wooly worm.
  • They eventually pupate into the Isabella tiger moth, which is much less colorful than their larval stage.
  • They are nocturnal.
  • Their lifespan ranges to about 1 year.
  • They generally weigh less than a gram, clocking in at about 0.68 g.
