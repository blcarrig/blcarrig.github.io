White-tailed Deer

    A herbivore that serves a key prey species for many of the ecosystems that it inhabits.
  • They inhabit much of the mixed forests of northern Mississippi.
  • Their lifespan can range up to 4.5 years.
  • They can grow to be 160 lbs.
  • Their top speed is a whopping 30 miles per hour.
  • They have a very notable sexual dimorphism like most deer, as males (bucks) have antlers that they shed while females (does) do not.
