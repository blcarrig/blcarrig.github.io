Bonnethead Shark

    These omnivorous cartilaginous fish inhabit much of the Gulf Coast region of southern Mississippi.
  • Their lifespans can range up to 9 years
  • They can grow up to 10 lbs and 59 inches in length.
  • They are in the hammerhead family of sharks, of which it is the smallest species.
  • They are often found in bays or estuaries.
