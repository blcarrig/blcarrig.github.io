    The American Alligator is a ferocious carnivore that resides in the wetlands of Northern Mississippi.
  • Its lifespan can be up to 40 years in the wild.
  • Its weight can be upwards of 450 lbs, with some growing even larger.
  • The American Alligator is an apex predator in its respective habitat, having no other animal that preys on it.
