Moccasin Snake

    These slithering carnivores have a notable pale mouth interior that gives them their common name.
  • Also known as a cottonmouth or water moccasin.
  • Their lifespan can range up to 12 years.
  • They can grow up to 2 lbs and 4 feet in length.
  • They are extremely venomous.
