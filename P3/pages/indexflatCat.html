Flathead Catfish

    These Mississippi staples are piscivores, meaning they eat mainly fish and other seafood.
  • Also known as a Shovelnose Cat.
  • Their lifespans can range up to 15 years.
  • They can weigh up to 57 lbs and grow up to 61 inches in length (Record Catch).
  • They have been introduced to many other regions in the U.S., but they are native to the Gulf Coast region.
  • They are nocturnal.
