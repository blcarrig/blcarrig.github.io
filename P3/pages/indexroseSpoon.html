Roseate Spoonbill

    These coastal piscivores eat fish, crabs, and other small coastal prey animals.
  • They are wading birds, meaning they use their long legs to wade into the surf and use their specialized beak to search for food.
  • Their lifespan can range up to 10 years.
  • They can grow up to 1.8 lbs with a wingspan of 47 to 53 inches.
  • They have a notable bright pink body that they get their name from.
