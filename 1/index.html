Thomas the Disgraced Knight

Thomas d Givras is one of the main characters in Between Two Fires by Christopher Buehlman.

He is a disgraced knigth who's also been excommunicated by the Church turned brigand during the time of the Black Plague.

    Things I Like About This Charcter:
  • Thomas is a complex character, wrestling with morality until late in the story.
  • He fights Revelations-inspired monsters in 1348 France.
  • He wears a big straw hat.
